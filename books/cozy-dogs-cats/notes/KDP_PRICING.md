## Is $8.99–$11.22 good for a ~100-page book?

**Yes.** Kids’ paperback coloring books commonly land around **$7.99–$12.99**.  
You’re not selling “50 pages of paper” — with blank backs you ship a **103-page** interior, which feels substantial on the shelf.

- **$8.99** — competitive / impulse  
- **$9.99** — sweet spot (recommended)  
- **$10.99–$11.22** — fine if cover + Bay Area positioning feel premium  
- Avoid going under ~$8 unless running a launch promo

Expanded Distribution (other retailers) drops royalty toward ~40% — profit shrinks a lot; turn that on only if you understand the tradeoff.

## What you pay Amazon to “publish”

KDP has **no upfront listing fee**. You pay when a copy sells (print cost comes out of royalty).  
Optional: proof copy shipping (usually a few dollars + shipping) if you order a physical proof.
