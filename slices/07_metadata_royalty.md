# Slice 07 --- Metadata and Royalty Calculator

## Outcome

Prepare publishing information and visible economics.

## Metadata

Structured fields for title/subtitle, description, keywords/categories
where applicable, author/imprint information, and book identifiers.

## Economics

Inputs should be configurable: - list price - printing cost -
marketplace/royalty assumptions

Outputs should clearly show the assumptions and estimated
royalty/profit. Do not bury mutable marketplace rules as unexplained
constants.
