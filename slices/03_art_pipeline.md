# Slice 03 --- Artwork Pipeline

## Outcome

Artwork can move through source → review → approved without ambiguity.

## Requirements

-   Separate source and approved art.
-   Record page/scene association.
-   Record approval state.
-   Support replacing one page without rebuilding source data.
-   Preserve original files.
-   Add checks for missing assets.

## Young-Child QC

Flag art that is obviously incompatible with: - thick outlines - large
open areas - simple shapes - minimal clutter - one clear
activity/subject
